# 🛡️ Computer Lab Safety Adventure

An interactive educational game designed for **Grade 5 students** to learn computer laboratory safety, responsible technology use, safe browsing, and basic digital citizenship through fun, game-based activities.

🎮 **Live Game:**  
[Play Computer Lab Safety Adventure](https://tharushadils.github.io/Safe_and_Fun_Use_of_the_Computer_Lab_-Simple_Game/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

---

## 📚 About the Project

**Computer Lab Safety Adventure** is a simple educational web game created to make computer laboratory safety lessons more engaging and interactive for young learners.

Instead of learning safety rules only through traditional notes, students can explore different scenarios, make decisions, earn points and stars, and receive feedback based on their choices.

The game focuses on:

- 🖥️ Physical computer lab safety
- 👍 Good and bad computer-use habits
- 🌐 Safe internet browsing
- 🔐 Protecting personal information
- 🛑 Stop – Block – Tell
- 👩‍🏫 Asking trusted adults for help

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing the game, students should be able to:

- Identify safe and unsafe behaviors in a computer laboratory.
- Understand why food and drinks should be kept away from computers.
- Handle computer hardware carefully.
- Keep bags and cables safely organized.
- Recognize potentially unsafe websites and online activities.
- Understand the importance of protecting personal information.
- Apply the **Stop – Block – Tell** rule when communicating with unknown people online.
- Ask a teacher or trusted adult for help when they encounter an online problem.

---

## 🎮 Game Levels

### ⭐ Level 1 – Physical Lab Safety

Students learn how to behave safely inside a computer laboratory.

Topics include:

- Keeping food and drinks away from computers
- Handling cables carefully
- Keeping walkways clear
- Taking care of computer hardware
- Asking a teacher for help when necessary

---

### ⭐ Level 2 – Good Habits vs Bad Habits

Students are presented with different computer-lab behaviors and must identify whether each behavior is a **Good Habit** or a **Bad Habit**.

Examples include:

- Sitting correctly at a computer
- Treating hardware carefully
- Following laboratory rules
- Avoiding unsafe actions

---

### ⭐ Level 3 – Safe Internet Explorer

Students learn basic safe-browsing skills through an interactive web environment.

They practice:

- Checking website links
- Identifying trustworthy websites
- Avoiding suspicious links
- Closing dangerous pop-ups
- Searching for educational information safely

---

### ⭐ Level 4 – Stop – Block – Tell

Students learn how to respond when an unknown person requests private information online.

The three-step rule is:

**1. 🛑 STOP**  
Do not reply or provide personal information.

**2. 🚫 BLOCK**  
Block the person from contacting you.

**3. 👩‍🏫 TELL**  
Tell a teacher, parent, or another trusted adult.

---

### 🏆 Final Boss – Safety Hero Gauntlet

The final challenge combines concepts from the previous levels.

Students must demonstrate their knowledge of computer laboratory safety and digital citizenship to earn the **Safety Hero** rank and collect all available stars.

## 📋 Computer Lab Golden Rules

The game teaches six important safety principles:

1. 🚫 **No drinks or food near computers**
2. 🔌 **Never pull cables by force**
3. 🎒 **Keep bags and walkways organized**
4. 🧘 **Be gentle with computer hardware**
5. 🔒 **Never share passwords or private information**
6. 🛑 **Follow the Stop – Block – Tell rule**
