📋 Computer Lab Golden Rules

Remember these 6 key safety principles every time you use technology:

1
🚫 No Drinks or Food near PCs

Spills cause electrical shorts, damage keyboards, and create shock hazards.

2
🔌 Never Pull Cables by Force

Ask Teacher Maya or a lab supervisor if a cord is tangled or stuck.

3
🎒 Store Bags in Racks / Walkways Clear

Keep lab floors clear so students don't trip while walking.

4
🧘 Be Gentle with Hardware

Never hit monitors or smash keys when a computer freezes. Stay calm and ask for help.

5
🔒 Never Share Passwords or Address

Keep your personal info private. Real websites will never ask for your home address randomly.

6
🛑 Stop – Block – Tell Rule

If an online stranger makes you uncomfortable: Stop talking, Block them, and Tell a trusted adult.
